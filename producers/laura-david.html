Terroir

Tuffeau limestone with flinty sand (silex) and clay over alluvial soils on the south bank of the Loire.

Founded in 2017 by Laura David, the domaine is based in Lussault-sur-Loire and spans 9 hectares in Montlouis-sur-Loire. The vineyards are planted on flinty sand and clay soils typical of the appellation, with Chenin Blanc as the sole variety.

Farming is carried out organically, with a focus on expressing the character of each site. Vinification takes place in a troglodyte cellar, where natural temperature and humidity allow for gentle fermentations with indigenous yeasts and minimal intervention, preserving clarity and precision in the wines.

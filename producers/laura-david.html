Troglodyte cellar wines from Montlouis-sur-Loire

Terroir
Tuffeau limestone with flinty sand (silex) and clay over sandy alluvial soils on the south bank of the Loire, between the Loire and Cher river systems.

Founded in 2017 by Laura David, the domaine is based in Lussault-sur-Loire and spans 9 hectares in Montlouis-sur-Loire. The vineyards are planted on flinty sand and clay soils typical of the appellation, with Chenin Blanc as the sole variety.

Farming is organic, with a focus on expressing the character of each site. Vinification takes place in a troglodyte cellar, where natural temperature and humidity allow for gentle fermentations with indigenous yeasts and minimal intervention, preserving clarity and precision in the wines.

Terroir
Tuffeau limestone with flinty sand (silex) and clay over sandy alluvial soils on the south bank of the Loire, between the Loire and Cher river systems.

Founded in 2017 by Laura David, the domaine is based in Lussault-sur-Loire and spans 9 hectares in Montlouis-sur-Loire.

Farming is organic, with natural fermentations in a troglodyte cellar using indigenous yeasts and minimal intervention.

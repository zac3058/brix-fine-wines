Troglodyte cellar wines from Montlouis-sur-Loire

Farming

Terroir
Tuffeau limestone with flinty sand (silex) and clay over sandy alluvial soils on the south bank of the Loire.

Founded in 2017 by Laura David, the domaine is based in Lussault-sur-Loire and spans 9 hectares in Montlouis-sur-Loire.

Farming is organic with minimal intervention vinification in a troglodyte cellar.
